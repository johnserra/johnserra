## My Story

I don't have a linear career path; I have a toolkit built across industries.

I've led the re-engineering of a patented manufacturing process for a national food packaging company. As Education Coordinator during the abrupt COVID-19 shutdown, I guided a language school from paper to a live online learning operation in a matter of weeks. Today, I develop new markets and strategic partnerships in the urban mobility space while building practical analytics and AI-assisted learning systems.

What connects all of it is a single discipline: looking at a complex situation, finding the signal in the noise, and building something that works.

## What I'm Building

My work sits at the intersection of real-world operations, clear data definitions, and AI-assisted engineering.

**Practical Small-Business Analytics:** On johnserra.com, I write step-by-step guides and help small and medium businesses turn messy spreadsheets and conflicting reports into dependable decisions—defining table grain, reconciling numbers, and keeping tool stacks simple.

**CareerTalkLab:** I built and run CareerTalkLab, a free open community for people growing in technical careers. Today it offers scaffolded A1–A2 Business English (built on four-stage Errorless Teaching) and weekly B1–C1 industry briefings, with our Grain First data analytics track in development.

**AI-Assisted Workflows:** I design and evaluate stateful LLM pipelines, deterministic validation checks, and grounded retrieval systems—treating AI as a force multiplier for human judgment rather than a black box.

## How I Work

I'm a multi-disciplinary problem solver by design. The most useful solutions usually come from connecting domain reality with clean data structure and testable systems.

My approach is straightforward: define the decision first, verify what the data actually represents, build something concrete, and check the output before trusting it.
