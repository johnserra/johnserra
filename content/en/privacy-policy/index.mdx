# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how johnserra.com collects, uses, and protects information when you visit or interact with this website.

## Who I Am

This is the personal website of John Serra. You can reach me at [john@serra.us](mailto:john@serra.us).

## Information I Collect

### Contact Form

When you submit the contact form, I collect your **name**, **email address**, and **message**. This information is stored in a database and delivered to me by email so I can respond. Service providers involved in storage and delivery are listed below. I do not sell this information or use it for marketing.

### AI Chat Widget

The chat widget on this site uses Google's Gemini API. Messages you send through the chat are sent to Google to generate responses. This site does not save your conversation across a page reload or store a chat transcript for later retrieval. It uses short-lived, hashed session and IP identifiers to limit abusive traffic. Please do not share sensitive personal information through the chat. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) for information about Google's processing.

### Server Logs

Like all websites, this site automatically logs basic request data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) through Vercel's hosting infrastructure. These logs are used for security and performance monitoring and are not linked to your identity.

## Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Vercel may set functional cookies necessary for the hosting infrastructure to operate.
